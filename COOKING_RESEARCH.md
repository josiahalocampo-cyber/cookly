No physical kitchen tests were performed. Displayed totals are estimates for two servings, not validated service promises. Prepared sauces, fully cooked legumes, boneless chicken where stated, and packet-led grain preparation are explicit assumptions.

Steak: the initial two-minute check is for browning a roughly 2–2.5 cm boneless steak. Flip, then begin basting after about one minute; basting is included in second-side cooking. Internal temperature controls completion. The default medium target stays at 63°C with at least three minutes rest to meet the cited safety recommendation; culinary labels vary.
